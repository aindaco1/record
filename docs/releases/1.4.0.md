# Record 1.4.0

Release candidate — not yet published.

Record 1.4 introduces a black-and-white app icon inspired by a 1980s
shoulder-mounted VHS home video camera and changes the default Area screenshot
shortcut for new installations to **Option-Command-Shift-4**.

Existing users keep their current shortcuts, including custom bindings, Off,
and the original **Command-Shift-4** default. To adopt the new shortcut, choose
**Settings… → Screenshots → Restore Defaults**, or edit only the Area shortcut.
Full Display remains **Command-Shift-1** and Window/Application remains
**Command-Shift-2** by default.

The new icon retains Record's black artwork and rounded white background. Its
editable source is `Sources/Record/Resources/AppIcon.svg`; all app-icon sizes
are generated with `scripts/release/generate-icon.sh`. That script also derives
the transparent menu-bar camera from the same source. Its 24-by-18-point
silhouette stays steady while a 5-point red recording dot blinks at the lower
right. Pausing hides the dot; Reduce Motion keeps it steady red. This applies
to both screen and audio-only recordings.

Capture, export, clipboard, transcription, and privacy behavior are unchanged.
