# Record 1.3.0

Record 1.3.0 adds a fast, local screenshot workflow for Apple Silicon Macs
running macOS 15 or newer.

Published September 2, 2026 from signed tag
[`v1.3.0`](https://github.com/aindaco1/record/releases/tag/v1.3.0) at source
commit `34db739d9d2c76d2cfae6fef4d15b38fef3e4ee2`. The public app is build 14.
The downloaded DMG and ZIP passed checksum, provenance, Developer ID,
notarization, stapling, Gatekeeper, and stable-TCC-identity verification. The
signed launch updater was also exercised from 1.2.3 to 1.3.0.

## Capture and continue

- Command-Shift-1 captures the full display containing the pointer.
- Command-Shift-2 opens Apple's selector for one window or an application's
  visible windows.
- Command-Shift-4 opens Record's area selector.

The shortcuts are editable in **Settings… → Screenshots** and can be turned Off.
Record reports combinations already owned by macOS or another app and links to
macOS Keyboard Shortcuts so the built-in screenshot bindings can be disabled
when desired.

Full-display and area capture request macOS Screen Recording access because
they directly capture display pixels. Window/application capture goes through
Apple's private picker and does not require that broader permission. All three
modes can include Record's own windows, including Record Settings; screen
recordings continue to hide Record to prevent visual feedback.

Every completed capture saves immediately into the existing approved export
folder and independently copies a lossless PNG to the clipboard. Disk files
default to native-resolution lossless PNG. Optional JPEG export defaults to
95% quality and flattens transparency onto white. Filenames use
`Screenshot YYYY-MM-DD at HH.mm.ss` with collision-safe numeric suffixes.

A brief menu-bar flash confirms complete success. The optional subtle CC0
shutter cue plays when pixels are captured and is automatically silent during
an active recording.

## One settings surface

**Settings…** now separates General, Screenshots, and Recording preferences.
General owns the one **Save to** destination shared by screenshots and completed
screen or audio recordings, capture-privacy exclusions, and Open at Login.
Screenshots owns still-image format, quality, feedback, and shortcuts. Recording
owns finished-name templates and local transcription configuration.

The menu remains action-focused: Screen source stays available for quick
changes; recent-recording, Gifski, and failed-transcription actions remain
contextual. **Open Recovery Folder…** replaces the misleading temp-session
label and appears only when private session material needs inspection.

## Local by construction

Screenshots reuse Record's native ScreenCaptureKit selectors, capture-privacy
rules, and security-scoped export-folder grant. Record stores no selected
window title, app name, screenshot history, preview, or edit document. It adds
no account, analytics, upload client, OCR, cloud processing, model download, or
main-app network entitlement. Sparkle's reviewed sandboxed services remain the
only update network path.
